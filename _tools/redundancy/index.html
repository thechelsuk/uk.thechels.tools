It works out **age-banded statutory redundancy pay** year by year, adds **notice pay** (the statutory minimum or your contract, whichever is longer) and **pro-rata holiday pay**, including days carried forward from a previous leave year. Weekly pay is capped at £751, service is capped at 20 years, and you need at least 2 complete years to qualify." - q: Is redundancy pay taxed in the UK? a: The first £30,000 of combined statutory and enhanced redundancy pay is free of tax and NI. Notice pay and holiday pay are taxed and NI'd as normal earnings. - q: Can I carry forward holiday I couldn't take because I was off sick? a: Yes. A large final payment in one month is taxed as if it repeats every month.
