Everything updates as you go. - Open **Pots and contributions** and **Pensions and income** to enter your own figures and ages. - Watch **Earliest viable age** and **Pots run out**, then switch to **Year by year** for the detail. about: | This is a quicker, more visual companion to the [FIRE calculator](/fire). It works in **real terms**: one growth rate after inflation, with pensions that keep pace with inflation, so every figure is in today's money. Your **workplace pension**, **SIPP** and **ISA** grow together, and once you retire they're drawn down in proportion to cover whatever your guaranteed income doesn't. A **defined benefit** pension and its tax-free lump sum (added to the ISA) start at the age you choose, and so does the **State Pension**. Spending can change at a later age, since many people spend less as they get older. **Earliest viable age** is the first retirement age at which the pots still have money in them at 100. Other income before retirement, such as a salary or sick pay, is shown in the table but isn't saved. It's a model with a steady return, not financial advice. faq: - q: Why are all the pots drawn down together? a: It keeps the model simple. In practice you might use the ISA first, before you can take your pension at 57 or 58. The [ISA Bridge calculator](/the-ISA-retirement-apex-bridge-tool) looks at exactly that. - q: What growth rate should I use? a: A real rate, after inflation. 4–5% is a common long-run assumption for a global equity portfolio; use less if you hold bonds or cash. related: [/fire, /the-ISA-retirement-apex-bridge-tool] ---
