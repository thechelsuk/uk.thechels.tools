--- title: Refinement Question Generator permalink: /refinement category: planning tags: [agile, refinement, facilitation] added: 2025-05-30 summary: Random thought-provoking questions to sharpen backlog refinement sessions. description: "Free backlog refinement question generator for agile and Scrum teams. A standing checklist plus three random questions to challenge each story." scripts: [app.js] how_to: - Work through the **Always ask** questions for every item you refine. - Press **New questions** for three random prompts to dig deeper. - Draw again for the next story, so the conversation doesn't get stale. about: | Refinement is where a team turns a vague idea into work it understands well enough to start. It goes wrong when the same people ask the same questions every week, or when nobody asks the awkward ones. The **Always ask** list covers the checks worth making for every item: whether it should be done at all, who needs it, data and security, architecture, performance and infrastructure. The random questions add variety: risks, assumptions, measures of success, dependencies and alternatives. Use it on a shared screen in the session, or let a different person draw the questions each time. faq: - q: How many questions should we use per story? a: The standing list plus three random ones is plenty for most items. Skip any that clearly don't apply. related: [/teaming, /monte-carlo] config: count: 3 always: - "Should we even be contemplating this work? Is there a quicker, cheaper or safer solution?" - "What are the actual user needs?" - "Are we exposing new data for the first time, and have we considered data protection and security?" - "Are we using a new pattern or technology that needs an architectural decision record?" - "What is the simplest way we could deliver value earlier?" - "What are the performance implications of this work?" - "How will this work affect the infrastructure, for example scaling?" questions: - "How could we deliver the value while doing the least amount of work?" - "What assumptions are we making that we should test?" - "What could go wrong if we implement this as described?" - "What could go wrong if we don't implement this as described?" - "Is there any missing information or ambiguity?" - "What are the risks of not doing this work?" - "What are the risks of doing this work?" - "How will we measure success?" - "What is the minimum viable product for this work?" - "What are the acceptance criteria?" - "How will we know when this is done?" - "Who are the stakeholders?" - "What are the non-functional requirements?" - "How does this align with our strategic goals?" - "What are the potential impacts on users?" - "What is the timeline for this work?" - "What resources do we need?" - "Who needs to be involved or informed?" - "Are there dependencies or blockers?" - "What are the edge cases?" - "How will we test this?" - "Is this the right time to work on this?" - "What are the alternatives to this approach?" - "How will this affect our current systems?" - "What is the long-term vision for this feature?" - "How will we handle user feedback?" - "What are the compliance requirements?" - "How will this work affect our technical debt?" - "What are the potential costs of this work?" - "How will we communicate progress to stakeholders?" ---
