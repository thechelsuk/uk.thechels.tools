Preview any hex code, convert it to RGB and HSL, check WCAG contrast against black and white text, and save colours to a palette." scripts: [app.js] how_to: - Type a hex code such as `005ca3` or `#fa0`, or use the colour picker. - Read off the RGB and HSL values and the contrast with white and black text. - Press **Save to palette** to keep a colour. Click a saved colour to load it again. about: | Hex codes describe a colour as three pairs of hexadecimal digits for red, green and blue. This shows the colour large enough to judge, converts it to the `rgb()` and `hsl()` forms CSS also accepts, and works out its contrast ratio with white and black text. The contrast figures use the WCAG 2 formula. Normal text needs at least **4.5:1** to pass level AA, and large text needs **3:1**. The preview uses whichever of black or white reads better. Your palette is saved in this browser's local storage, so it's still there next time but isn't shared with anyone. faq: - q: What's a three-digit hex code? a: A shorthand where each digit is doubled, so `#fa0` is the same as `#ffaa00`. - q: Where is my palette stored? a: Only in this browser. Clearing site data or using another device starts a fresh palette. related: [/slug, /image-to-svg] ---
