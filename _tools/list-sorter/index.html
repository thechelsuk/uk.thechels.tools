--- title: List Sorter permalink: /list-sorter category: text tags: [lists, sorting, text] added: 2025-03-12 summary: Sort a list A–Z or Z–A, reverse it, and remove duplicates. description: "Free online list sorter. Sort lines alphabetically or in reverse, with natural number ordering, duplicate removal and whitespace trimming, in your browser." scripts: [app.js] how_to: - Paste your list into **Input**, one item per line. - Choose an order, and tick **Remove duplicates** or **Trim spaces** if you need them. - Copy the sorted list. about: | Sorting is case-insensitive and understands numbers, so `item 2` comes before `item 10` rather than after it, the way a person would expect. Blank lines are dropped. **Reverse** keeps your original order and just flips it, which is handy for turning a newest-first list into oldest-first. Nothing you paste leaves your browser. faq: - q: Why does "item 10" come after "item 9"? a: The sorter compares numbers inside text by value, not character by character. A plain character sort would put "item 10" before "item 2". - q: Does it keep my original capitalisation? a: Yes. Case is ignored for ordering only. related: [/string-sorter, /extract-urls] ---
