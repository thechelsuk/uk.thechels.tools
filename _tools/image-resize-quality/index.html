Click any preview to see it full size. - Download the smallest one that still looks right. about: | Choosing a JPEG quality by number is guesswork; seeing the results side by side isn't. This makes ten versions of your image, at full and half width and at 100%, 90%, 70%, 50% and 30% quality, and shows how big each file is. Halving the width usually cuts the file to about a quarter of the size, which matters far more than quality for images that are shown small. If your image has transparent areas, choose a **Background** colour to fill them, since JPEG can't store transparency. Your image is processed in the browser and never uploaded. faq: - q: Why is quality 100% so much bigger than 90%? a: The top end keeps detail that's almost impossible to see. 85–90% is usually indistinguishable at a fraction of the size. related: [/image-to-jpeg, /image-to-webp, /exif] ---
