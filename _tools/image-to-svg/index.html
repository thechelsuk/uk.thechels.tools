Posterised and smoothed styles give smaller, cleaner files; detailed keeps more of the original. - Download the SVG, or copy its code. about: | Bitmap images are made of pixels and go blurry when enlarged. An SVG describes shapes instead, so it stays sharp at any size. Tracing turns areas of similar colour into shapes, which works best for logos, icons, drawings and other images with flat colours. Tracing is done by the open source [imagetracerjs](/sbom#imagetracerjs) library by András Jankovics, running in your browser. Photographs trace into thousands of shapes and very large files, so start small, or use a posterised style to limit the colours. Your image isn't uploaded anywhere. faq: - q: Why is my SVG so big? a: Every patch of colour becomes a shape. Try a posterised or smoothed style, or a simpler image. - q: Can I edit the result? a: Yes.
