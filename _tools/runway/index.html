Leave any you don't use at £0. - Enter each pot's interest or growth rate, and your tax rate on savings interest. - Enter what you'll spend each month and press **Calculate runway**. - Read how many months it lasts, then check the chart and the month-by-month table. about: | After redundancy, a settlement or an inheritance, the question is often simply "how long will this last?". This spends a fixed amount each month and works out how long your money keeps going while the rest of it earns interest. Money is drawn in the most tax-efficient order: the **taxable savings account** first, then **Premium Bonds**, then the **Cash ISA**, and the **Stocks and Shares ISA** last, so the wrapper with the highest growth and no tax is kept longest. Interest on the savings pot is taxed at the marginal rate you choose; the others are tax-free. Everything is in today's money, with no allowance for inflation. Premium Bond "interest" is your chosen average prize rate, assuming average luck, and the Stocks and Shares ISA uses one steady return, which real markets never deliver. Treat it as a guide, not a forecast or financial advice. faq: - q: Why are savings spent first? a: Interest on savings can be taxed, but ISA and Premium Bond returns aren't. Spending the taxable money first keeps more of your money growing tax-free. - q: What if my investments grow faster than I spend? a: The calculator stops after 100 years and tells you the money would last indefinitely at those rates. related: [/uk-redundancy-pay-calculator, /fire] ---
