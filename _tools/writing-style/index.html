Replace them with a number or cut them. - **Passive voice**, like *the test was run*, hides who did something. *We ran the test* is shorter and clearer. - **Repeated words**, like *the the*, are easy to miss when a line breaks between them. The passive voice check is a rule of thumb: a form of *to be* followed by a word ending in *-ed* or a common irregular past participle. It flags some false positives, such as *he is tired*, so read each one rather than changing it automatically. Your text stays in your browser. faq: - q: Is passive voice always wrong? a: No. It's useful when the actor doesn't matter or isn't known.
