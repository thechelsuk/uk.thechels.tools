--- title: Extract URLs permalink: /extract-urls category: text tags: [links, text] added: 2025-03-12 summary: Pull every link out of copied web content or plain text, one per line. description: "Free URL extractor. Paste content from a web page, email or document and get every link as a list, with duplicates removed, in your browser." scripts: [app.js] how_to: - Copy part of a web page, an email or a document, and paste it into **Input**. - Links behind text, like a "read more" link, are picked up as well as URLs written out in full. - Tick **Remove duplicates** if you only want each link once, then copy the list. about: | When you paste from a web page, your clipboard carries the page's links as well as its words. This reads those links, plus any `http` or `https` address written in the text itself, and lists them one per line. It's useful for gathering the sources in an article, checking where the links in an email really go, or turning a bookmarks page into a plain list. The pasted content is read as data and never run, and nothing leaves your browser. faq: - q: Why are some links missing? a: Only `http` and `https` links are listed. Email (`mailto:`) and in-page (`#section`) links are skipped. - q: Can I paste plain text? a: Yes. Any web address written out in the text is found too. related: [/list-sorter, /escape-entities] ---
