--- title: UK FIRE Retirement Calculator permalink: /fire category: finance tags: [fire, retirement, pension, finance] added: 2025-04-30 summary: Will your savings last from early retirement to 100, alongside a final salary and State Pension? description: "UK FIRE calculator. Project your portfolio to retirement, then year by year to 100 with a defined benefit pension and State Pension, lower spending from 75, and inflation." vendor: [chart.js] scripts: [app.js] how_to: - Enter your age, when you'd like to retire, and what you'll spend each year before and after 75. - Add your current investments, monthly contributions, and expected growth and inflation. - Tick and fill in any defined benefit (final salary) pension and the State Pension. - Press **Calculate** to see whether the money lasts to 100, and how your income is made up over time. about: | FIRE, "financial independence, retire early", is about building enough investments to live on before the usual retirement age. This projects your portfolio year by year: growing with contributions until you retire, then drawing down whatever your pensions don't cover, until you're 100. Many people spend less in later life, so spending can step down from age 75. A **defined benefit** pension (from a final salary scheme, for example) and the **State Pension** reduce what you need to take from your investments once they start. Tick **today's money** to see every figure adjusted for inflation. The **FIRE number** is the classic rule of thumb: your yearly spending divided by a safe withdrawal rate, such as 4%. The year-by-year projection is the fuller picture. This is a model with steady returns, not financial advice. faq: - q: What's a safe withdrawal rate? a: The share of your portfolio you could take each year with a good chance of it lasting. 4% comes from US research over 30-year retirements; many people use 3–3.5% for longer UK retirements. - q: Should I use real or nominal growth? a: Enter the expected market return as growth and inflation separately. Ticking today's money then shows everything after inflation. - q: What State Pension should I enter? a: Check your forecast at gov.uk/check-state-pension, and use your State Pension age, which is 67 or 68 for most people under 60. related: [/fire-two, /the-ISA-retirement-apex-bridge-tool, /uk-runway-calculator-drawdown] ---
