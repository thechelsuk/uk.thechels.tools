--- title: Rolling Average Chart permalink: /rolling-average category: planning tags: [charts, averages, data] added: 2025-03-14 summary: Chart a series of numbers with its rolling average and overall average. description: "Free rolling average calculator. Paste a series of numbers, choose a window size, and chart the moving average against the overall average." vendor: [chart.js] scripts: [app.js] how_to: - Paste your numbers, separated by commas, spaces or new lines. - Choose how many values each rolling average should cover. - The chart and table update as you type. about: | A rolling (or moving) average smooths out noise so the trend is easier to see. Each point is the average of the last few values, so a 3-point rolling average at week 10 is the mean of weeks 8, 9 and 10. The line starts once there are enough values to fill the first window. It's handy for sprint throughput, cycle times, weekly sales or anything else that bounces around. A wider window gives a smoother line that reacts more slowly to change. The dashed line shows the overall average for comparison. Your figures stay in your browser. faq: - q: What window size should I use? a: Start with 3 to 5 for weekly or sprint data. Use a wider window if the line is still jumpy, or a narrower one if it hides real changes. related: [/monte-carlo] config: sample: "5, 7, 4, 8, 6, 9, 7, 10, 8, 11, 9, 12" ---
