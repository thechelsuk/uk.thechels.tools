Decode QR codes from an image, a pasted screenshot or your camera, and check the link or text inside before you open it." vendor: [jsqr] scripts: [app.js] how_to: - Drop an image containing a QR code, click to choose one, or paste a screenshot. - Or press **Use camera** and hold the code up to it. - Read what the code contains. Links are only opened if you click them. about: | QR codes on posters, parking meters and emails can point anywhere, and scam codes stuck over real ones are common. Reading a code here shows you exactly what's inside first: the full web address, Wi-Fi network name, email address or text. Only `http` and `https` addresses become links, and they open in a new tab only when you click. Anything else, including scripts hidden in a code, is shown as plain text and never run. Decoding uses the open source [jsQR](/sbom#jsqr) library by Cosmo Wolfe. Images and the camera feed are processed in your browser and never uploaded. faq: - q: Why can't it find my QR code? a: Try a sharper or closer image with the whole code in view, including the white border round it. - q: Does it keep the camera on? a: Only while you're scanning. It stops as soon as a code is found or you press Stop. related: [/qr-generator, /exif, /extract-urls] ---
