Weeknotes are a short blog post about your week: what happened, what you learned and anything worth passing on. Writing one every week builds a record of your work and makes it easier to share progress with your team.

This is the Markdown template I start each one from. Copy it into a new post, then fill in each part.

## What goes in each part

- **Title and date:** the post title, with the week's date filled in by the `[[date]]` placeholder.
- **Summary:** a sentence or two on how the week went, where `<|summary>` sits.
- **Highlights:** three bullet points for the main things that happened.
- **Learning:** something new you picked up this week.
- **Picks:** an article, podcast, tool or anything else you'd recommend.

## The template

```markdown
# WeakNotes


WeekNotes [[date]]

<|summary>

-
-
-


learning

-

Picks

-
```
